01 Profile

Rachit Ratnam Mudaliar

is a Software Systems student at Kauno Kolegija in Kaunas, Lithuania.

Born in Birsinghpur Pali, Madhya Pradesh, and shaped by two years at a military school in Dehradun, he moved nearly 6,000 km from his hometown to study in Europe. Since the age of four he has asked one question of every system he meets: how does this actually work? Today he answers it by taking systems apart, and building his own.
