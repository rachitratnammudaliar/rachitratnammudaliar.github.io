About

Curious about the logic behind everything.

I'm a Software Systems student at Kauno Kolegija in Kaunas, Lithuania. I've wanted to know how things work since I was four, when my mother sat me at a computer and taught me MS Paint and how to type my name. That curiosity never left. I still take a system apart until I find the logic behind it, and then I build something of my own.

I grew up in Birsinghpur Pali, a small town in Madhya Pradesh, India, and spent two years at a military school in Dehradun. In 2026 I set out to build a life from scratch, first in Kerala and then in Lithuania.
