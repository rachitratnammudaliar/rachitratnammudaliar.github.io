About

Curious about the logic behind everything.

When I was four, my mother sat me at a computer and taught me MS Paint and how to type my name. I didn't just want to use it. I wanted to know how it worked. That question has followed me ever since.

I grew up in Birsinghpur Pali, Madhya Pradesh, India, spent two years at a military school in Dehradun, and in 2026 moved first to Kerala and then to Lithuania to build a life from scratch and study software.
